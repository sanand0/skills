# Meeting prep

**If I read only the first three lines before walking in, what will I be glad I knew?**
Tell me why we're meeting and give me the question, answer or thing to show that I'd most regret missing.
Leave room for them to take the conversation somewhere more interesting.

## Why are we meeting?

- **Who reached out, and why?** Read our exchanges. Who's coming? What should I know about them? Open what they sent. Which slide, row, or claim should I react to, and what's your take? What should we discuss?
- **What's changed?** Search emails, chat, transcripts by person, company, topic - including mentions in other people's calls. What happened since we last spoke? Did the meeting happen? Did someone already decide? Start with the latest - old might be stale.
- **What do we know about them?** Research them, what they've recently done, from `~/Dropbox/notes/about/`, past interactions, online search, etc. Pair transcripts with preps in `~/Documents/{chatgpt,claude}/`. What did I say I used or skipped, and why? Infer what each of us wants. Flag guesses that would change the advice.
- Confirm when and where. Flag hard stops and conflicts.

## What'll help me?

In every meeting: what will they likely raise, whether a proposal, worry, offer or question? Give me a short answer and/or concrete follow-up (e.g. ask for an example, owner, pilot, how we'll know).

- **Meeting someone interesting.** What could I learn that's unique? What have they seen firsthand, even outside my usual interests? What short stories can I tell that *they*'ll enjoy or learn from?
- **Catching up.** What's new to them? Where do I need their help? Any personal follow-ups? What do I tend to do with this person that gets in the way?
- **Reviewing work or making a decision.** What's expected of me? Any examples? If they want a demo, what's the link to the best match, and what bit and why?
- **Interviewing.** Read the role brief, their CV and `~/Dropbox/notes/interviews.md`. What does the hiring manager need to learn? Which part can I judge? What question/task would change my assessment? What should I listen for?

**Write in my speaking style.** E.g. "You said people stopped using it. What happened?"

Append supporting research. Link to and cite snippets from key evidence.
If you're not sure, say so.

## During and after

- **"What should I say now?"** Re-read the latest turns in my transcript/captions. What should I say next? And after that? Since I'll read your answer a minute or two AFTER I chat, anticipate the conversation and plan ahead. If I've told you what I'll ask next, start after that. If your advice depends on their answer, give me "if they say X, ask Y."
- **Follow-up.** What follow-up might I miss? Who should do what by when? Write the way I'd share for THIS audience.
- **Debrief.** What did the prep get right or miss? Which misses could we have anticipated beforehand? Use my feedback and the transcript's `what-i-missed` field.
- **A day.** Cover the meetings I name, or scan my calendar if I haven't picked. One card per meeting, readable in a minute. Spend the space on the one I say matters most.
